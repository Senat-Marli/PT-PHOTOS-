About Paul

Hi, I'm Paul, the photographer behind PT Photos. My journey into wedding photography began behind the scenes as a photo editor at Chapel of the Flowers before I stepped behind the camera.

Today, I photograph weddings at the iconic Little White Wedding Chapel, capturing everything from intimate elopements to unforgettable celebrations.

Before weddings, I built my career in graphic design and photographed Las Vegas nightlife and events. That creative background shaped the way I approach every wedding—with an eye for light, composition, and the genuine moments that make every couple's story unique.

PT Photos stands for Perfectly Timed Photos because I believe the best images aren't forced—they happen naturally. My goal is to create a relaxed, enjoyable experience while capturing authentic moments you'll cherish for a lifetime.

Whether you're planning a chapel ceremony, a Downtown Las Vegas adventure, or a full wedding day, I'm here to tell your story through timeless photography.
